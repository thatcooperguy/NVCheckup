About this example
------------------
This report is a SIMULATED capture. It was hand-assembled from the GB10 scenario that drives the
"Linux field test (simulated GPU)" CI job (.github/fieldtest/scenarios/gb10.json, spec section 10):
shims answer nvidia-smi, lspci, dpkg, lscpu, dmidecode, fwupdmgr, ibdev2netdev and
nvidia-spark-ota-check, and /etc/dgx-release, /etc/fastos-release, DMI, meminfo and thermal fixtures
are injected under NVC_SIM_ROOT. Two deliberate deviations from the healthy scenario were added so
that the report shows a WARN with an Advisory step: port 0 of the ConnectX-7 is cabled but
unaddressed (cx7-up-no-ip), and a CUDA 12.8 PyTorch wheel is installed (arm64-cuda12-wheel-on-cuda13,
which also produces the benign capability-12.1 INFO). Values such as MemTotal 125513944 kB, the
0000000F:01:00.0 bus id, the [N/A] memory fields, firmware versions and package names come from
public field reports cited in docs/roadmap/spark-support.md; idle clocks, the swap size and the
DKMS line are placeholders. Nothing here has been verified on hardware yet. A real capture is
tracked in https://github.com/thatcooperguy/NVCheckup/issues/3; scripts/spark-capture.sh
produces one.
